Common questions

Know what to expect.

Does Anvesh guarantee survey rewards?

No. Any reward depends on the published study terms and the reward functionality actually enabled for that study.

Does AI decide what the results mean?

AI can assist with drafting or interpretation where configured, but generated suggestions are not guaranteed to be correct and should be checked against the underlying response data.

Is every feature available today?

Anvesh is actively being developed. Availability can differ by workflow; the interface should identify when a capability is not configured rather than imply it has completed work.
