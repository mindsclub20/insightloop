A clearer process

Make research a process, not a pile of tabs.

Keep the research question, the method, the supporting evidence and the resulting report connected in one understandable flow.

STEP 01

Frame the question

Describe the problem and the decision the research should inform.

STEP 02

Build the plan

Define research questions, audience, methods and source strategy.

STEP 03

Gather evidence

Organise relevant sources and keep findings linked to their origins.

STEP 04

Review the report

Make findings, methodology and limitations visible before acting.

Designed for thoughtful decisions

A workspace for the whole research journey.

Research is the main concept. Surveys and questionnaires remain useful methods for collecting primary evidence when appropriate.

⌕

Research planning

Bring the problem, audience, questions and intended outcome together before you begin.

↗

Evidence and sources

Keep source material separate from summaries and interpretations, with room to note gaps.

▤

Reports and review

Organise findings, methodology and limitations into a report people can evaluate.

◎

Primary research

Use surveys as one supporting method rather than the entire research process.

◇

Clear status

Distinguish proposed work from running, completed, partially completed or failed work.

⌑

Privacy-aware design

Keep access role-aware and avoid showing unnecessary participant personal details.
